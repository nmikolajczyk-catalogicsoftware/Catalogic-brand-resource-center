Product Family

Catalogic's on-premises data protection suite consists of three tightly integrated products. DPX is the core backup and recovery engine. vStor is the software-defined backup repository built on ZFS. GuardMode provides proactive ransomware detection and cyber resilience. Together they form a unified solution for secure, reliable, cost-effective data protection.

DPX

Secure Data Protection

Enterprise backup and recovery with patented block-level protection. Reduces backup time by 90% while supporting physical, virtual, and cloud workloads. Covers Microsoft 365, Hyper-V, VMware, NDMP, bare metal, and tape.

vStor

Software Backup Appliance

Software-defined secondary storage built on ZFS. Provides deduplication, compression, immutable snapshots, encryption at rest, and point-to-point replication. Deploy on bare metal or as a virtual appliance on any block storage.

GuardMode

Proactive Ransomware Protection

Cyber resilience layer that detects ransomware early through blocklist matching, entropy analysis, honeypots, and access threshold monitoring. Identifies affected files, traces infection sources, and enables surgical recovery.

Usage Guidance

Blue (#2D3494) is the dominant color across all three product logos and should carry through to headlines, navigation, and primary UI elements in product materials.

Green (#8BBE41) is an accent. It appears in the DPX icon chevrons, the vStor "lid," and the GuardMode shield. Use it sparingly for call-to-action buttons, success states, and highlights. Never use green as a background color for text. The green variants (#92BB26, #82BC45) are specific to their respective logo assets and should not be mixed between products.

Contrast ratio: Blue on white passes WCAG AA for both normal and large text. Green on white does not pass AA for normal text; use it only for large text, icons, or decorative elements.

Do
✓
Always capitalize product names correctly

"DPX" is always all-caps. "vStor" starts with lowercase "v" then capital "S". "GuardMode" is one word with capital G and capital M. These apply everywhere: website, decks, documents, email.

✓
Prefix with "DPX" on first external mention

Write "DPX vStor" and "DPX GuardMode" on first reference in customer-facing materials. After that, "vStor" and "GuardMode" alone are acceptable.

✓
Use Blue (#2D3494) as the dominant brand color

Catalogic Blue is the primary color for all three products. Use it for headlines, navigation, and primary buttons. Green is an accent only.

✓
Use white logos on dark or busy backgrounds

Over photography, dark UIs, or colored backgrounds, switch to the all-white version of each logo for readability.

✓
Include "by Catalogic" in partner/co-brand contexts

When DPX logos appear alongside OEM or channel partner logos, add the Catalogic parent brand for attribution.

✓
Maintain clear space around each logo

Leave a clear area equal to the height of the "D" in "DPX" around all edges of any product logo.

Don't
✗
Don't miscapitalize product names

"Dpx", "dpx", "Vstor", "VSTOR", "Guardmode", "GUARDMODE" are all incorrect. Follow the exact capitalization rules above.

✗
Don't use green as a text color on white

Green (#8BBE41) on white does not meet WCAG AA contrast for body text. Use it only for icons, large display text, or decorative elements.

✗
Don't stretch, rotate, or skew logos

Always scale proportionally. Use the provided SVG files which maintain correct aspect ratio.

✗
Don't mix green variants between products

The DPX green (#8BBE41), vStor green (#92BB26), and GuardMode green (#82BC45) are specific to their logo assets. Don't swap them.

✗
Don't separate the icon from its wordmark

The DPX stacked-chevron icon, vStor disk icon, and GuardMode shield should only be used standalone when the product name is already established in context (e.g., app favicons).
